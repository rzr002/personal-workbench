# Privacy review policy

The automated scanner detects common secret shapes, private keys, email addresses, phone-like identifiers, absolute user paths, profile records, and session records. It cannot determine whether ordinary prose reveals confidential business rules, customer context, unpublished strategy, or proprietary evaluation data.

Before publishing, manually ask:

1. Could an outsider identify a person, employer, customer, host, repository, or internal system?
2. Does the change reveal a private workflow that should remain a registered local module?
3. Is every example synthetic and clearly generic?
4. Are paths relative or placeholder-based?
5. Did any candidate text become executable instructions without human review and tests?
6. Does `git status` contain only intended public files?
